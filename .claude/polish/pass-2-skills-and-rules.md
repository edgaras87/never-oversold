## Goal

The skills and rules say what this project actually lived. Where
the work went differently from what a skill or rule says, the copy
is corrected, or the difference is handed to the deliverer.

## What it looks for

- A step the skill skips, or one it asks for that never earned its
  place.
- A rule that was worked around.
- A word used in two senses.

The evidence: the devlog's DEAD END lines, PLAN's revisions, the
decisions log, and the commit plans' close bodies.

## What may be edited, and how

- **Ours, edited directly:** CLAUDE.md, the slice-record shape,
  `evidence-test.md`, new entries in `.claude/decisions.md`.
- **Delivered copies** (the skills, `delivered-copies.md`,
  `shapes-lifecycle.md`), under `delivered-copies.md` rule 2: each
  edit comes from something that happened in this project; one
  decisions entry per copy; one line per copy under *To the
  deliverer*. The next delivery lands each copy whole, so an edit
  the deliverer declines is gone then.
- `docs/concept/` stays untouched, as in every pass.
